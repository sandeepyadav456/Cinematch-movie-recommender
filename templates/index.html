    How this works

    Every film's overview, genres and tagline are combined into one text profile, weighted with TF-IDF, and compared by cosine similarity. The result is a ranked list of the closest-matching profiles — a form of content-based filtering that needs no viewing history to work.

    Data: The Movies Dataset (Kaggle)
